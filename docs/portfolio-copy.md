## Launch post draft

I wanted an English-learning routine that connects to things I already care about: personal projects, career decisions, and everyday challenges.

That led me to English Tutor. The public prototype now has a 50-session learning roadmap and one detailed lesson you can try in your browser. It guides a learner through reading, writing, phrase practice, and preparation for a separate ChatGPT Voice conversation.

My contribution has been defining the requirements, choosing the learning priorities, and refining the flow through personal use. GPT/Codex helped with content, prompts, and implementation. In the latest update, I focused on making the sample easier to enter and preserving written drafts after refresh.

It is a personal prototype: the local checker covers selected patterns, and B2 is my learning goal rather than a demonstrated outcome. The public app does not call an LLM API.

Try it: https://shved21.github.io/english-tutor/
Source and project story: https://github.com/shved21/english-tutor

I would welcome specific feedback: where does the writing exercise feel clear, and where does it need better support?
